# Output spec

## The brief

About six pages, written so a 15-year-old can follow it, in sentences that join related
ideas with commas and semicolons, never a string of short five- or six-word sentences. The headings and the length
are set in the profile and checked in code.

## The program design options list

One row per option: theme, option, organization, design, target group, where it
runs, delivery partner, evidence level, evaluation, outcome and effect, cost per
outcome, inclusion reach, transfer note, ACET's role, funders, posture, relation to
YES, and verification.

## The funder map

One row per funder: fit score, themes and priority countries matched, strategy and
period, instruments, typical size, whether ACET is eligible, and open calls.

## House style

- US English, active voice, and the serial comma.
- Spell out zero to nine, and use digits for 10 and up.
- "Percent" in prose, % in tables.
- US dates, such as June 20, 2026.
- Million, billion, and trillion written out, as in $145 million.
- No em dashes or en dashes.
- No sentence that sets one idea against another.
- Spell out each acronym the first time.
- Author and last modified: Kayode Adeniyi.
- No trace of any tool in any text, cell, or file property.
